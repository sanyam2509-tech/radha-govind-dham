Return & Refund Policy

Radha Govind Dham, Chail | Last updated: January 2026

Kripalu Padma Trust, operating as Radha Govind Dham, Chail (“we”, “our”, “us”) believes in transparency and honesty in all financial dealings. This Return & Refund Policy explains our position regarding payments, donations, and registrations made through our website.

By making any payment on our website, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined below.

1. General Policy

Radha Govind Dham, Chail does not offer returns or refunds for any payments made through the website, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

All payments are considered final and made in good faith.

2. Donations

Donations made to Radha Govind Dham, Chail are voluntary and non-refundable.

Once a donation is successfully processed, it cannot be cancelled or refunded under any circumstances.

We do not entertain refund requests due to change of mind, incorrect amount, or personal reasons.

3. Sādhana Shivir Registration

Registration for Sādhana Shivir requires payment of an advance amount to confirm participation.

The advance amount is collected to manage logistics, accommodation, food arrangements, and event preparation.

Important Notice

The Sādhana Shivir registration fee and advance amount are strictly non-refundable.

No refunds will be issued for:

  • Cancellation by the participant
  • Non-attendance or late arrival
  • Personal, medical, travel, or weather-related reasons
  • Change of schedule or personal circumstances

Participants are requested to register only after being fully certain of their availability.

4. Event Cancellation or Changes

In the unlikely event that Radha Govind Dham, Chail cancels the Sādhana Shivir due to unforeseen circumstances (natural calamities, government restrictions, or other unavoidable reasons), the decision regarding refunds or adjustments shall rest solely with the management.

Any communication regarding such changes will be made through the contact details provided at the time of registration.

5. Payment Gateway Issues

All online payments are processed through third-party payment gateways.

We are not responsible for:

  • Failed transactions
  • Duplicate debits
  • Technical errors caused by the payment gateway or banks

In case of a successful debit but failed registration confirmation, participants may contact us with valid transaction details for verification.

6. No Cash Returns or Adjustments

No cash refunds will be issued.

Payments cannot be transferred, adjusted, or carried forward to any future event or program.

7. Acknowledgement of Policy

By proceeding with any payment or registration on our website, you confirm that:

  • You have read and understood this Return & Refund Policy
  • You agree to the no-refund nature of donations and Sādhana Shivir registrations

8.Technical Errors & Duplicate Transactions

If a transaction is processed twice due to a technical error, or if the amount is debited but the registration is not successful, a refund will be initiated. The user must contact us within 48 hours with proof of transaction. Valid refunds will be processed within 5-7 working days and credited back to the original source of payment.

9. Contact Information
